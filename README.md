# JMC ScanVan

[V81]

The JcJenson Motor Division, in co-partnership with Vandal Electronics, are proud to (yet again) announce, and sell you, yesteryear's antique technology. The 'all-new for 2428' ScanVan!
Riding on the equally as dated backbone of Halden Electronics Company Cruiser, this hunk of metal is a 2-door, luton-style van with an enclosed roof, packed with plenty of extra & immersive features for you to play with!
This diesel & oil-burning relic can be purchased from the Terminal for the low-low price of `440` credits. Don't miss your chance to experience a corporations attempt at an "ideal fleet van" from 2003!
Equipped with technology 'borrowed' from the Crystaller Motor Corporation, among many others, a dated four-speed manual transmission, an inline-6 diesel engine that should be in a museum and a stubborness to die, you'd think this thing gained immortality.
But alas, corporate greed and questionably dodgy paperwork are the main things keeping this thing 'on-the-road'.

Thunderstore link: https://thunderstore.io/c/lethal-company/p/Scandal/Vandal_Electronics_Scanvan/

## Feedback

## Features
* Three seats
* Manual gearbox with clutch
* Enclosed roof - protects against various outdoor enemies.
* Functional gauge cluster (speedometer, tachometer, dashboard warning lights, etc)
* Functional center & side view mirrors
* A functional live radio (RadioFurniture in your van!) (Disabled by default, disable the Public-Domain Music config to enable this)
* Electric windows, interactable sunvisors, interactable hazard warning lights, etc!
* Six radio tracks
* Service manual, courtesy of Glojam, who made the Company Hauler mod
* Functional climate control (LethalElements compatability)
* ...other things?

## Compatibility

## Mods that will cause issues/do not work with the ScanVan:
- BetterEmotes (use MuchBetterEmotes by Seechela instead)
- CruiserSkin
- CruiserMap
- LCVR (this will be implemented in the future)

## Not compatible, but should not break or cause issues if installed:
- LategameCompanyCruiserUpgrades
- CruiserImproved
- BetterVehicleControls
- CruiserAdditions
- CompanyCruiserSteeringFix (wouldn't even be needed anyways)
- BetterCruiserSync
- 2 Story RV-Cruiser

*Some mods listed may have partial or full self-implementation, such as BetterVehicleControls keybinds, or configs such as automatically centering steering wheel.

### Compatible:
- LethalElements (working blower-motor/heater)
- CruiserTunes (you must disable the Public-Domain Music config in-order to listen to your own music.)
- ImmersiveVisor
- LethalMin
- FirstPersonView
